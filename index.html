About Me

Designing experiences that connect users with products

I'm a UI/UX Designer based in Lahore, Pakistan, creating intuitive digital products across SaaS, healthcare, fintech, marketplaces, and B2B/B2C platforms. I take ideas from user research and information architecture through wireframes, interaction design, and high-fidelity Figma prototypes.

My approach balances user needs, business goals, accessibility, and technical constraints. I collaborate closely with clients and development teams—including international US and UK stakeholders—to make sure designs are practical, consistent, and implemented accurately.

  • End-to-end design for web and mobile applications
  • Strong collaboration with development teams
  • Focus on usability and visual consistency
